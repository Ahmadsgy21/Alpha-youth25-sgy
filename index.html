About Us

ALPHA YOUTH 25 SABON GARIN YAYA is a community youth association based in Sabon Garin Yaya, Taura Local Government, Jigawa State, Nigeria.

Our focus is to support students in educational matters and related issues while encouraging unity, responsibility, knowledge and progress.

Our Objectives

Education

Support students with educational guidance, encouragement and relevant assistance.

Youth Development

Encourage useful skills, confidence and responsible leadership among young people.

Community Service

Participate in activities that contribute positively to Sabon Garin Yaya.

Unity

Promote cooperation, respect and peaceful relationships among members and the community.
